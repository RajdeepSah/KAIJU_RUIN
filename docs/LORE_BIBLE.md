*Codename: Kaiju Ruin. This is the canon-reference spine for the game — the single place the design brief, character/kaiju art, story mode, and Horrific Endings check their facts against. It fulfils the lore-bible half of **Vision Goal 4 / milestone M4**.*

*Last updated: 2026-07-25 (session 8). Owner-reviewable draft.*

## 0. What this document is (and is not)

**Authority.** VISION.md is the owner-authored source of truth for *why the game exists* and carries the original **Research Digest** (Vision §3), synthesized from the official site copy for the seven published *Realm of Goryo* works plus genre context. This Lore Bible **restructures and consolidates that digest into a working canon reference** — timeline, factions, characters, kaiju, peoples, places, folklore, tone — with every entry tagged for confidence so art and design never mistake an inference for a fact.

**Hard limit on v1.** This v1 is built from the **Vision Research Digest only**. The actual novels and the official press kit have **not** been read into it. Vision §3.5 and §12 require a proper lore pass *"built from the official press kit and the novels themselves, reviewed with the author"* before character design hardens. **v1 is therefore a structuring pass, not that deep-source pass.** It invents no new canon: where the source is silent, this document says so rather than filling the gap. A v2 folds in the books/press kit and the author's review, at which point `[INFERENCE]` entries are confirmed or corrected and `[OPEN]` questions are answered.

**Relationship to Pillar 1.** *"The novels are the engine"* (Vision §6, Pillar 1) and ground rule 2 (*canon is law*) govern this file. Nothing here overrides VISION. Any conflict between this document and VISION is a bug in this document.

## 1. Confidence legend

Every substantive claim below is tagged:

- **`[CONFIRMED]`** — stated in the official copy as reported by the Vision Research Digest (Vision §3.5 "Confirmed from official copy"). Safe to build on.
- **`[INFERENCE]`** — an educated read (folklore pattern, taxonomy, genre convention) not stated in the source. Design *may* use it, but keep it **revisable** and flag it in the asset/design record. All character and kaiju **appearances and fighting abilities are `[INFERENCE]` by default** (Vision §3.5).
- **`[OPEN]`** — a known gap the source does not answer; needs the novels/press kit or the author. Do not design a hard dependency on an `[OPEN]` answer.

## 2. Setting & timeline

- **`[CONFIRMED]`** The present of the saga is **2061**. By 2061, **Japan has been overrun by kaiju**; threats loom *"from the bottom of the ocean to distant planets."* Humanity operates at **human scale inside a monster-scale crisis** — the founding image of the whole IP.
- **`[CONFIRMED]`** The world **unites to reclaim the planet** while scientists and soldiers in Japan work carefully **to avoid triggering a world war**.
- **`[OPEN]`** The full timeline — how long the crisis has run before 2061, the interval between books, and the sequence of the later escalations — is not established by the source.

### The saga in reading order (the canon spine)

Seven published works. Each is `[CONFIRMED]` to exist with the beats summarized; the *causal detail between* beats is largely `[OPEN]`.

1. **The Four Pillars** — `[CONFIRMED]` 2061; Japan overrun; the world unites to reclaim Earth; Japan's scientists and soldiers move carefully to avoid a world war. *The game's story mode begins here (Vision §5).*
2. **The Culling** — `[CONFIRMED]` The world has barely settled *"since the rise of Mu"* when a new threat approaches. To prepare the world, a figure named **Tengi must first cull it** — *a protector whose method is mass harm*. This is the series' moral signature.
3. **Baptism** — `[CONFIRMED]` Cities destroyed; recovery failing; *"millions left voiceless in a world that is leaving them behind."* Kaiju once contained in Japan **spread across the globe**. New dangers rise in **Mu** and feed an insurrection. Theme: the abandoned and the unheard.
4. **The Riven Mother** — `[CONFIRMED]` The world braces for **Raisha, the Riven Mother**. **Arikura** urges the UN and Mu to stand down. **Noah Sato** is on the run with the werefox **Kest** toward the **kaiju graveyard**, where **Kest intends to unleash Raisha** and usher in a new era for the kaiju. Human and caudata **prepare together** (→ caudatas are a coequal people).
5. **Arms Race** — `[CONFIRMED]` Drums of war; shadow actors maneuver for victory; a new **fear-spreading threat** rises in Mu. In **Anquisheng**, the caudatas' dream of freedom becomes a nightmare as kaiju descend on their home. Noah Sato hides *"from both man and monster."*
6. **Monster in the Machine** — `[CONFIRMED]` *"The king is dead, and the world is about to change."* Hidden in Tokyo, **Noah Sato builds the Ark**, *"a world of forever,"* the next stage of civilization — a dream *"born from a world that abandoned him and nurtured by the alien terrorist **Satori**,"* whose own plan is to destroy the old world and build a new one *"on the backs of the kaiju."* — **`[OPEN]` Which king died is not stated.**
7. **Khulandra Attacks!: Make Your Escape** — `[CONFIRMED]` An **interactive choose-your-path book**. The kaiju **Khulandra** rises from the depths and attacks the city; the reader survives or dies through *"dozens of horrific endings."* Tagline: *"Goryo won't save you this time."* → establishes Goryo as the realm's **guardian kaiju `[INFERENCE, strongly implied]`** and gives the game its **Horrific Endings** DNA (Vision §5).

## 3. Forces of the world (factions)

Four-to-five factions emerge cleanly — the structure a fighting-game roster and story mode want (Vision §3.2).

| Faction | Confidence | What it is | Game read |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The World Coalition / The Four Pillars** | `[CONFIRMED]` | The UN, the united governments, and the Japan-based scientists and soldiers of *The Four Pillars*. Humanity reclaiming Earth without igniting a world war. | Coalition ace, Arikura (defensive), the institutional "human scale" POV. |
| **Mu** | `[CONFIRMED]` risen power / `[INFERENCE]` name origin | A risen power; source of **insurrection** and **fear-wielding** new threats. The name *almost certainly* nods to the lost Pacific continent of pulp/kaiju cinema `[INFERENCE]`. | A Mu **dread-officer** (fear as a mechanic). |
| **Anquisheng & the caudatas** | `[CONFIRMED]` people / `[INFERENCE]` salamander-folk | An **oppressed amphibian people**; *Anquisheng* is their home. "Caudata" is the taxonomic order of **salamanders**, so they are *very likely* salamander-folk `[INFERENCE]` — a distinctive, ownable species. | A **caudata vanguard** from Anquisheng. |
| **The Ark circle** | `[CONFIRMED]` | **Noah Sato's** transhuman project (the Ark, *"a world of forever"*) under **Satori's** apocalyptic patronage. Technology as refuge *and* threat. | Noah Sato (Ark-tech trickster), Satori (mind-reading zoner). |
| **The kaiju ascendancy** | `[CONFIRMED]` | Those who want the **monsters to inherit the Earth** — **Kest** foremost. | Kest (transforming werefox rushdown). |

**Above all factions stand the kaiju** — forces of nature and objects of belief, not roster picks (Vision §3.2, Pillar 2).

## 4. The kaiju (weather, not costumes)

Governed by **Pillar 2**: kaiju are *environment, catastrophe, deity, and endgame* — never a health bar the size of a human. In-game they appear as **living stages, raid-boss Sieges, and ultimate summons** (Vision §5), never as ordinary opponents at launch.

- **Goryo — the guardian.** `[CONFIRMED]` named kaiju. **`[INFERENCE, strongly implied]`** the realm's *guardian* (from *"Goryo won't save you this time"*). Name ← **goryō**, a vengeful/venerated spirit (§7). **`[OPEN]` visual canon.**
- **Khulandra — the destroyer from the depths.** `[CONFIRMED]` rises from the depths and attacks the city (the *Make Your Escape* kaiju). **The slice's living-stage event** (harbor tide-surge). **`[OPEN]` visual canon** (placeholder design is inference).
- **Raisha — the Riven Mother.** `[CONFIRMED]` the world braces for her coming; **Kest intends to unleash her** at the kaiju graveyard to begin a new era for the kaiju. **`[OPEN]` visual canon, nature of her "riven" motherhood.**
- **The kaiju graveyard sleeper.** `[CONFIRMED]` a **kaiju graveyard** exists and is where Raisha is unleashed. **`[OPEN]`** whether anything else *"sleeps"* there is Vision framing, not confirmed detail.

### 5.1 Slice roster (locked for the vertical slice — see §9)

**Kest — the werefox.**
- `[CONFIRMED]`: a **werefox**; travels **with Noah Sato**; is **on the run toward the kaiju graveyard**; **intends to unleash Raisha** to usher in a new era for the kaiju (i.e., aligned with the **kaiju ascendancy**, in tension with Sato's own aims). A **werefox is a kitsune** by another name `[CONFIRMED as naming, INFERENCE as depiction]`.
- `[INFERENCE]` (placeholder design, ASSET_MANIFEST): part-transformed kitsune — fox ears, feral eyes, clawed hands, tattered traveling cloak, **spectral teal fox-fire**; lean, androgynous, rushdown build.
- **Fighting-game read** `[INFERENCE / proposal]`: agile transforming **rushdown**; fox-fire dashes and phantom-claw rakes; the "more action / faster" identity (D-015: `AttackSpeed` 1.12, teal foxfire accent).
- `[OPEN]`: canon appearance; the nature of Kest's transformation; Kest's relationship to Sato (ally, foil, both?); canonical abilities.

**Tengi — the culler.**
- `[CONFIRMED]`: in *The Culling*, **Tengi must cull the world to prepare it** for a coming danger — *a protector whose method is mass harm.* The series' moral signature.
- `[INFERENCE]`: **Tengi echoes tengu** (naming pattern; treat as inference). Placeholder design (ASSET_MANIFEST): towering heavy in **dark lacquered plate**, **tengu-mask helm**, crow-feather mantle, a massive single-edged **culling blade**.
- **Fighting-game read** `[INFERENCE / proposal]`: grim **heavy** whose theme is *the culling*; broad, committed, slower swings; crimson bladewave accent (D-015: `AttackSpeed` 0.92).
- `[OPEN]`: canon appearance; faction allegiance in detail; whether the tengu read is intended; canonical abilities; the "coming danger" Tengi culls against.

## 8. Tone & the M-rating (how canon becomes Mature)

- `[CONFIRMED]` The **mainline novels** are praised for *"clean language"* — **dark and lethal in event, restrained in depiction.** The **gamebook** (*Make Your Escape*) trades in *"dozens of horrific endings."*
- **Design consequence:** an MK-style **M rating is a deliberate escalation, not a neutral translation** (Vision §3.4), and it is made **with the rights holder** (approved internally per D-009). The escalation is **earned through the Horrific Endings** — authored, inked comic **splash panels** with weight — not through dismemberment sport (Vision §5, Pillar 4).
- **Hard lines (Pillar 4, absolute regardless of rating):** no sexual violence; no torture presented as reward; no real-world extremist iconography; **nothing involving minors.** Gore that does not serve **fear or story** is noise and is cut. Violence must **communicate** (whose hit, how hard, what changed) before it decorates.
